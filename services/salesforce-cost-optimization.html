Common Savings Opportunities

Inactive User Licenses (25-40% savings)

Most organizations have 25-40% of licenses assigned to inactive users—former employees, seasonal workers, or dormant accounts. At €100/user/month, 20 ghost licenses = €24,000/year waste.

Edition Over-Provisioning (20-35% savings)

Companies often start with Enterprise Edition "just in case" but use only <5% of advanced features. Downgrading eligible users to Professional can save 20-35% per license.

Forgotten AppExchange Apps (€10K-€100K/year)

Organizations subscribe to 10-15 AppExchange apps, actively use 3-4. Forgotten apps cost €500-€5,000/month each. One audit client discovered €78,000 in unused app subscriptions.

Storage Overages (€20K-€100K/year)

Salesforce charges €3,000+/month for each TB over allocation. Simple data archival and attachment cleanup can cut storage 40-70% with zero data loss.
