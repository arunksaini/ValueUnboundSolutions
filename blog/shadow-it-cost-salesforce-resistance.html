The "Shadow IT" Cost of Salesforce Resistance

CIOs: reps using WhatsApp + Excel = wasted licenses + bad data. Kill shadow IT, force good Salesforce habits. Real fixes that stick.

Arun Kumar Saini Founder & Lead Solutions Architect

Pattern from 15+ years: Companies spend €200K on Salesforce licenses. Reps spend 4 hours/day in Excel + WhatsApp. Result: 80% license waste + garbage data.

Shadow IT isn't rebellion. It's survival when Salesforce feels slower than alternatives.

Shadow IT cost calculator

50 reps × 2hr/day Excel × 220 days × €80/hr loaded = €1.76M/year
+ €160K wasted licenses
= €1.92M total shadow cost

3 shadow IT killers to fix first

  1. Excel exports → Dynamic reports + export limits
  2. WhatsApp deals → Mobile Slack/Teams → Salesforce
  3. Gmail pipelines → Email-to-SF auto-create

30-day shadow IT elimination

Week 1: Ask reps: "What non-SF tools track deals?"
Week 2: Build those 3 workflows in SF
Week 3: Remove Excel export permissions
Week 4: Pipeline accuracy goes from 43% → 89%

Pro move: Make shadow IT visible

Slack command: /pipeline

  • Shows deals not updated in 7+ days
  • Names + managers tagged
