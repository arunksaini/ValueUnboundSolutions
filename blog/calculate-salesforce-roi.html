Salesforce ROI: Prove It or Kill It

Heads of Sales: prove Salesforce drives revenue or face budget cuts. Simple formula + benchmarks from 15+ years real implementations.

Arun Kumar Saini Founder & Lead Solutions Architect

CFO question I hear everywhere: "What revenue does Salesforce actually drive?"
Answer most teams give: "It's strategic!" (Translation: "We don't know.")

Here's the dead-simple ROI formula that works across industries.

The 3-number Salesforce ROI

Revenue generated through Salesforce / Total cost = ROI

Example:
€2.1M revenue tracked ÷ €210K spend = 10x ROI

Number #1: Revenue through Salesforce

Opportunities created in SF × Win rate × Avg deal size
85 opps/mo × 28% × €25K = €595K/mo = €7.1M/year

Number #2: Total cost (not just licenses)

Licenses €153K + Consulting €72K + Apps €35K = €260K

Number #3: ROI ratio CFOs love

7.1M revenue ÷ 260K cost = 27x ROI

Benchmarks:

  • Poor: <5x
  • Good: 10-20x
  • Excellent: 25x+

  • <20% win rate = sales process broken
