The Salesforce Complexity Tax – You're Paying It

CIOs/CIOs: every custom object/flow doubles maintenance cost. Spot complexity waste, simplify fast. 15+ years real projects.

Arun Kumar Saini Founder & Lead Solutions Architect

Rule from 15+ years: Every hour spent simplifying Salesforce saves 3 hours of future maintenance.

Most companies don't see complexity as a cost because it doesn't have a SKU. But it kills: €50-€200K/year in hidden consulting, delays, and frustration.

Complexity cost calculator (run this)

  • Custom objects: 15 × €8K/year = €120K
  • Custom fields: 80 × €1K/year = €80K
  • Flows: 25 × €4K/year = €100K

Total "tax": €300K/year

5 complexity killers to cut first

  1. Fields nobody updates (delete 50% safely)
  2. Duplicate processes (lead → opp → case flows)
  3. Abandoned automations (last run >90 days)
  4. 100+ page layout variations (standardize to 3)
  5. Custom code nobody understands (rewrite as Flow)

Your "complexity diet" – 30 days

Week 1: Count custom objects/fields/flows
Week 2: Tag "last used >90 days"
Week 3: Delete 20-30% safely
Week 4: Document keepers, standardize layouts

Business impact numbers

Before: 8 week change requests
After: 2 week change requests
Result: 6 weeks faster time-to-market
