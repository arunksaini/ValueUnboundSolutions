Salesforce Storage Overages – The Silent Budget Killer

CFOs: €10K-€50K annual storage overages hit quietly. How to audit, clean, archive in 2 hours. Real fixes from 15+ years experience.

Arun Kumar Saini Founder & Lead Solutions Architect

Quick math: 1TB Salesforce storage = €3K+/month overage. Most companies pay this without knowing why.

I've helped multinationals cut storage 40-70% without losing data. Here's the exact process business leaders use to kill overages.

Where storage waste hides (check these first)

  1. Attachments (old proposals, 10MB PDFs) = 60%
  2. Chatter files (screenshots nobody needs) = 20%
  3. Marketing images/emails in rich text = 10%
  4. Duplicate files (same doc in 3 places) = 10%

Your 2-hour storage cleanup playbook

Hour 1: Diagnose

  • Setup → Storage Usage
  • Download: Top 20 space hogs
  • Check: Last Modified dates
  • Flag: Anything >2 years old

Hour 2: Clean

  • Download old attachments to Google Drive/SharePoint
  • Mass-delete Chatter files >1 year
  • Find duplicate Opportunities/Contacts
  • Archive inactive Leads to external system

Expected savings: 30-60% storage footprint.

The "never pay overage again" system

  • Q1: Clean attachments
  • Q2: Archive old records
  • Q3: Delete Chatter junk
  • Q4: Pre-renewal deep clean

Pro tip: Negotiate storage into renewal

"We've reduced usage 45%. Can we lock current rate for 24 months?"
