The Salesforce "True-Up" Trap – How to Avoid Paying for Ghosts

Salesforce "true-ups" catch 80% of companies off-guard. How to audit usage yourself and avoid surprise license bills.

Arun Kumar Saini Founder & Lead Solutions Architect

I've been through dozens of Salesforce renewals across multinational companies in India and Europe. The moment that kills trust every time? The "true-up" email that arrives 30 days before renewal: "Based on your usage, you owe us €45K for 28 extra licenses."

In every project where we avoided this, we didn't beg for discounts. We pre-cleaned usage 90 days early. Here's exactly how business leaders take control of true-ups.

The true-up trap in 3 sentences

  1. Salesforce tracks actual usage (logins, API calls, features)
  2. Your contract says "pay for the higher of seats OR usage"
  3. Most companies discover this when renewal quotes land 20-50% higher than expected

Real pattern I've seen: 85 seats → 112 "usage" = instant €32K bill.

Your 60-minute true-up prevention checklist

Print this. Do it today:

  • Export: Users + Last Login Date (last 12 months)
  • Count: 30+ days inactive (deactivate)
  • Count: 60+ days inactive (downgrade license)
  • Check: Shared logins (each needs own seat)
  • Check: Service accounts counting as "users"
  • Export: Permission Set Licenses (these true-up too!)

Expected savings: 8-15% of total license spend, instantly.

3 conversations to have RIGHT NOW

With your Salesforce admin:
"Show me the last login report. Who are the bottom 20% users?"

With department heads:
"These 12 people haven't logged in 90+ days. Do they still need access?"

With Finance:
"Put a recurring calendar invite: 'Salesforce cleanup Q3 every year.'"

When true-ups are legitimate (don't fight these)

  • New sales reps hired last quarter
  • Legitimate feature expansion (Service Cloud rollout)
  • API usage spikes (new integration went live)

Pro move: Document these as "business growth." Negotiate from strength.

The "true-up proof" renewal script

"We've cleaned up 14 inactive seats and 3 shared logins already. Our true usage is now 98 seats. What's the quote for exactly what we use?"

Realistic numbers from my experience

Before cleanup: 112 "usage" seats
After cleanup: 98 actual seats
Result: €28K saved on true-up
